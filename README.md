# Magnetocardiography-101
A beginner-friendly resource hub for Magnetocardiography (MCG), covering basics, data examples, and analysis scripts.

Welcome to my personal research space in Magnetocardiography (MCG).

## 👨‍🔬 About Me
I am [yaya6639], a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Information Engineering at Beihang University, Beijing, China. With a solid foundation in clinical medicine (Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Cardiology), my research now focuses on signal processing, sensor systems, and clinical applications of magnetocardiography (MCG) for early diagnosis of cardiovascular diseases.

My mission is to bridge clinical cardiology needs with advanced sensing and computational technologies.

## 🧭 Purpose of This Repository
1.  To curate and share fundamental knowledge, open datasets, and practical code related to MCG.
2.  To document my learning and research journey, building reproducible analysis pipelines.
3.  To foster interdisciplinary dialogue among clinicians, engineers, and students entering the field.

## 📂 Repository Structure (Under Active Development)
*   `docs/` – Literature notes, reviews, and tutorials.
*   `data/` – Example datasets and preprocessing scripts.
*   `tools/` – Signal processing and feature extraction utilities.
*   `notebooks/` – Interactive Jupyter notebooks for demonstration and education.
